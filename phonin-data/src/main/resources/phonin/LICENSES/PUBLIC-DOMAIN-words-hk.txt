The full words.hk dictionary CSV is not bundled here because it is distributed only
on request via https://words.hk/faiman/request_data/. To enable Cantonese word-level
data, obtain the CSV, convert it to a TSV (headword<TAB>jyutping), place it at
build/downloads/wordshk/words.hk.tsv, and re-run the pipeline.
